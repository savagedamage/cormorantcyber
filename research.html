The Underground Economy

Where the money actually moves in breaches, extortion, fraud and exploit sales — and what the same map looks like from the defending side.

figures: public reports, cited per line window: 2025 reporting + 2026 to date no client data · no invented numbers

The market split in two

Underground economics in 2025–26 stopped being one market. The commodity middle — stolen cards, basic web bugs — collapsed on oversupply and AI automation. The money concentrated at two poles.

Pole one — bespoke capability

Hard-to-replace access, sold quietly

Chains, footholds and extortion leverage that take real skill and can't be mass-produced.

  • Zero-click mobile chain$20M
  • iOS / WhatsApp chains$7–8M
  • Verified corporate access (avg)$113K
  • Median ransom (up 368% YoY)$59.5K
Pole two — social engineering at scale

Manipulation, industrialized

No exploit needed. Impersonation, pressure and AI-made realism, run against thousands of targets.

  • Investment fraud (US, 2025)$8.65B
  • Business email compromise$3.05B
  • AI-enabled fraud (deepfakes, voices)$893M
  • Avg BEC incident$123K
Collapsing middle: a reflected XSS is worth ~$68, Internet Bug Bounty criticals were cut 76%, and stolen card data keeps getting cheaper. AI tools flood the low end; brokers and big-game operators buy the high end.

The same map, from the defending side

Every hot segment above creates a matching defensive budget. This is where buyer demand sits right now — mapped from the same public data, not from sales folklore.

Where the budgets are

  • Crypto-security IR — post-Bybit, exchanges and protocols are the highest-stress buyers on the market.
  • Access & stealer defense — phishing-resistant MFA, session hygiene, credential-exposure hunting.
  • Deepfake / BEC controls — finance teams losing six figures per incident fund verification processes.
  • AI & LLM security — the one bug-bounty category still rising (+540% prompt-injection reports).

The entry surface

Defensive work scales from the same skills as the research side: reading software for what actually ships.

  • Application security review — Android, web, API; fixed scope, written findings, evidence per claim.
  • Published method — the public research corpus is the credential.
  • Before/after proof — same read-only collectors, run twice, delta reported honestly.
